Variation Design Details: 
Collapse the filters (Zone, Light Requirement, etc.) by default. There will be two variants:
Variant 1: All filters are collapsed by default.
Variant 2: All filters are collapsed except for the "Zone" filter, which remains expanded since it receives the most filter engagement.
Behavior: When a user selects a filter option from a collapsed filter section, keep that filter section expanded. The selected filter should remain open even after the user refreshes the page.
